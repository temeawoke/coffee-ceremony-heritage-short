# Film Design — The Ethiopian Coffee Ceremony

## 1. Project overview
- **Film title:** The Ethiopian Coffee Ceremony
- **Target duration:** 70 seconds
- **Narration:** Amharic
- **Subtitles:** English
- **Delivery formats:** Vertical (9:16) and horizontal (16:9)

## 2. Primary audience
Ethiopian families and the young generation.

## 3. Audience need
Introduce younger viewers to the meaning and sequence of the Ethiopian coffee ceremony, while giving families a short cultural story they can share.

## 4. Film purpose
Show that Ethiopian coffee is more than a drink: the ceremony is a practice of hospitality, connection, sharing and cultural continuity.

## 5. Intended audience experience
Viewers should recognise the main stages of the ceremony and understand why gathering around coffee matters.

## 6. Creative direction
Use warm, natural light; close details of beans, roasting, grinding, the jebena and cups; and respectful depictions of family and guests. Keep the visual style consistent across shots. Avoid text generated inside images; add any titles in the editing stage.

## 7. Radio-cut audience test
The first prototype will contain Amharic narration, quiet placeholder music and a black screen. Show it to at least three people representative of the primary audience. Record their actual comments, any timing or pronunciation issues, and the changes made to the storyboard below.
