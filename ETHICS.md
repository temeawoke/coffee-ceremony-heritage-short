# Ethics, consent, labelling and licences

## Real-person likeness and voice
The initial storyboard is written as a general cultural depiction. If identifiable real people or their voices are introduced, obtain and record informed consent before generation or publication.

## AI labelling
Disclose realistic AI-generated or altered content on publishing platforms as required. Keep provenance metadata and watermarks intact where available.

## Licences
Record the model, version, licence/terms, date checked and any restrictions for every provider and generated asset.

## Privacy
Do not submit private family information to services unless their data-use terms are understood and appropriate consent has been obtained.
